Official agency marks used by the mobile "Explore Space Agencies" section.

Files are loaded as /agency-logos/<agency-id>.svg (then .png as a fallback) and
shown in white via a CSS filter, so any logo with a transparent background works.
Included: nasa.svg, isro.svg, spacex.svg (Simple Icons, CC0 artwork of the brands' marks).
Add esa.svg|png, jaxa.svg|png and cnsa.svg|png here to replace the text fallback
that is shown for those three agencies until the official files are provided.
